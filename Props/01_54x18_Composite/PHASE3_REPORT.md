# Phase 3 report: parametric geometry

Generated by `make_phase3_report.py` from `results/phase3.json`. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. Summary

- `src/geometry.py` turns the Phase 2 blade table into a two-blade, one-piece propeller with a hub disc: STEP (mm) for manufacturing and
  STL (m) for CFD, in **CCW and CW** versions. Everything is generated from `params.yaml` and `results/blade_final.csv`.
- **Both surfaces are watertight** by two independent checks (my own edge count and OpenFOAM `surfaceCheck`), and feature edges are extracted for meshing.
- **Handedness verified from the finished STL, not from the generator's intent:** the CCW prop's leading edges point counter-clockwise, the CW
  prop's clockwise, and both have nose-up pitch and camber toward +z, so **both push air down when turned their own way**.
- Smoothing the blade table for lofting changed hover RPM by +0.06 % and power by -0.01 % (BEMT), i.e. nothing.
- Chord and twist measured back from the STL agree with the definition to within 0.19 % and 0.28°.

## 2. How the geometry is built

**Axes.** z is the rotor axis and thrust is +z. Blade 1 lies along +x. The CCW prop turns counter-clockwise seen from above, so blade 1 moves
toward +y and its leading edge faces +y.

**Sections.** NACA 44xx from the analytic 4-digit formulas (41 cosine-spaced points per surface, small blunt trailing edge). A section at
radius r with chord c and twist θ is placed as

    P = (r, 0, 0) + (x_stack − xc) c (0, cos θ, sin θ) + yc c (0, −sin θ, cos θ)

so positive twist lifts the nose toward +z and the cambered (suction) side faces +z.

**Spanwise smoothing.** The BEMT table has corners where the chord cap and root rules start. A monotone cubic (PCHIP) through 14 of its
stations gives a C1-continuous chord and twist with no overshoot. Between 0.18 R and 0.985 R the largest changes are 3.1 mm in chord and
0.11° in twist.

**Tip.** Outboard of 0.985 R the chord is rounded off elliptically to 50 % of its value and closed with a flat cap.

**Root.** Inboard of 0.18 R the shank narrows to 1.3 × the hub radius (65 mm), its stacking point moves from
0.25 c to 0.45 c so it sits centred on the hub, and the section thickens from 18 % to 30 %. My first version kept the full chord to
the hub and left the trailing edge sticking out past the hub as a thin fin; the render caught it and this transition replaced it.
The loft starts inside the hub (at 0.6 × hub radius) so the boolean union has real overlap.

**Hub.** A plain disc, radius 50 mm, height 45.5 mm (sized to enclose the root section). **No bore, bolt circle, keyway or
root fillet yet**: those are the §4.4 hub design in Phase 5, and a fillet at the blade-hub junction should be added then.

**CAD.** A generated gmsh script lofts 30 sections with OpenCASCADE `ThruSections`, copies the blade by a half turn, unions both with the
hub and writes the STEP file and a surface mesh. CadQuery is not installed, so gmsh's OpenCASCADE kernel does the same job.

**CW version.** A mirror image in the x-z plane (y → −y), generated from mirrored section points. A mirror is required: no rotation of a CCW
prop produces a CW one.

**Handedness (the brief's right-handed mirror check)**

| Prop | Rotation for positive lift | Thrust direction | Same at all five stations | BEMT thrust from the measured geometry at the design hover RPM [N] | vs design thrust |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CCW | CCW (counter-clockwise from above) | +z | yes | 419.2 | +0.96 % |
| CW | CW (clockwise from above) | +z | yes | 419.1 | +0.94 % |

A blade makes positive lift only when it moves nose-first, so the side the leading edge faces fixes the rotation sense. CW vs CCW: thrust differs
by -0.016 %, volume by +0.0005 % and polar inertia by -0.0002 % (criterion A13 asks for thrust within 1 %; the real test of
A13 is CFD). The +1.0 % against the design thrust comes from the twist measurement bias above.

## 5. Problems and notes

1. **OpenFOAM cannot run in this project folder**: it aborts on any path containing spaces. Cases are generated and run under
   `~/OpenFOAM/elijah-callahan-13/run/prop54x18` and their logs and results are copied back. This is a folder outside the project, created in this phase.
2. The blade-hub junction is a sharp intersection with no fillet, and the hub has no mounting features. Both belong to Phase 5.
3. The trailing edge is the natural blunt edge of the NACA formula (0.14 mm thick at 75 % R). A minimum manufacturable thickness is not applied yet.
4. About 0.2 % of the STL triangles are slivers at the tip cap and trailing edge. `surfaceCheck` accepts them; snappyHexMesh may need care there.
5. Sections lie in flat planes x = r rather than on cylinders, the usual convention. At the root the difference is not negligible, but
   that region carries almost no load.

## 6. Next: Phase 4.0, Caradonna–Tung validation
